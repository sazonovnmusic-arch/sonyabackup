## Search Strategy That Worked

1. Search queries: `"[franchise] theory dark"`, `"[franchise] hidden details"`, `"[franchise] secret you missed"`, `"[franchise] mind blowing"`, `"[franchise] detail missed"`
2. Parameters: `order=viewCount`, `videoDuration=short`, `publishedAfter=2025-01-01T00:00:00Z`
3. Filter: keep only videos with franchise name in title (removes irrelevant hits)
4. Batch `videos.list` (50 IDs per call) to get stats — filter by duration ≤60s and views ≥50K
5. **API QUOTA WARNING**: 300+ search queries in one session exhausted the daily quota (429). Batch searches across sessions or limit to ~80 queries per session.
